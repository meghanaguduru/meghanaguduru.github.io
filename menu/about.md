Hey! I’m Meghana. I’ve spent the last 7 years as a Machine Learning and Computer Vision engineer at [Meta](https://about.meta.com/), diving deep into AI, Computer Vision, and Virtual/Augmented Reality. I've had the thrill of contributing to iconic products like [Meta Quest 1, 2, 3](https://www.meta.com/quest/) and [Portal](https://www.meta.com/portal/).

I'm a hackathon junkie—there’s nothing like the rush of building something from scratch, turning wild ideas into impactful solutions that can make a real difference. I thrive at the intersection of AI/ML, Computer Vision, NLP, and prototyping, bridging the gap between software and hardware to create tech that matters.

My journey began with a Master’s in ECE from [Carnegie Mellon University](https://www.cmu.edu/) in 2017, where I first fell in love with teaching computers how to see. Before that, I earned my B.Tech in E&ECE from [IIT Kharagpur](https://www.cmu.edu/), laying the groundwork for my tech passion.

Outside of work, you’ll find me soaking up nature, playing with kids, reading, or hacking away at a side project. Lately, I’ve been diving into yoga and exploring a deeper understanding of myself—because growth isn’t just for code; it’s for life too.
